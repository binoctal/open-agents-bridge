# Trademark Policy / 商标政策

## English

"Open Agents", "OpenAgents", "open-agents-bridge", the associated logo and the
official domains (openagents.top and its subdomains) (the "Marks") identify the
official software and services published by the maintainer (binoctal). The
source code is licensed under AGPL-3.0; that license covers the code and does
not grant any right to use the Marks.

### Permitted use (no permission needed)

- Stating the facts: "compatible with Open Agents", "works with the Open Agents bridge".
- Linking to and describing the official project, in articles, tutorials and reviews.
- Using the name unmodified to refer to the official project or an unmodified official release.
- Redistributing unmodified official release binaries under their original name.

### Forbidden use

- Using the Marks as the name, logo or domain of your own product, fork, package, extension, app or service.
- Anything that suggests your product, server or release is official, endorsed, certified or operated by the maintainer.
- Names or domains confusingly similar to the Marks (typosquatting, added words like "official", "pro", "plus").
- Running a control plane or distributing builds that present themselves as the official service.
- Using the Marks to solicit credentials, payments or pairing codes.

### Forks and modified versions

- A fork or modified build MUST use a different name and logo and MUST NOT use the official domains or package names.
- You MUST NOT imply it is official. State clearly that it is an unofficial fork of Open Agents.
- Modified builds must not carry the official-build flag; official release signatures apply only to releases signed by the maintainer's key (see docs/release-signing.md).
- You must keep the AGPL-3.0 license and copyright notices.

### Reporting and contact

Suspected impersonation or confusingly similar names can be reported to the
maintainer (contact channel to be filled in by the owner before publication).
The maintainer may ask for removal of infringing names, domains and packages.

## 中文

"Open Agents"、"OpenAgents"、"open-agents-bridge"、相关 logo 以及官方域名
（openagents.top 及其子域名）（合称"商标"）用于标识维护者（binoctal）发布的官方软件与服务。
源代码以 AGPL-3.0 授权，该授权只覆盖代码，不授予任何使用商标的权利。

### 允许的使用（无需许可）

- 陈述事实，例如"兼容 Open Agents"、"可配合 Open Agents bridge 使用"。
- 在文章、教程、评测中链接并介绍官方项目。
- 原样使用名称指代官方项目或未修改的官方发行版。
- 以原名称再分发未经修改的官方发行二进制。

### 禁止的使用

- 将商标用作你自己的产品、fork、软件包、扩展、应用或服务的名称、logo 或域名。
- 任何暗示你的产品、服务器或发行版是官方的、获得维护者认可、认证或由其运营的行为。
- 与商标易混淆的名称或域名（抢注近似域名，或加 "official"、"pro"、"plus" 等字样）。
- 运营自称官方服务的控制面，或分发自称官方的构建。
- 利用商标诱导他人提供凭据、付款或配对码。

### Fork 与修改版

- fork 或修改版必须使用不同的名称和 logo，不得使用官方域名与包名。
- 不得暗示其为官方版本，须明确声明"这是 Open Agents 的非官方 fork"。
- 修改版构建不得携带官方构建标记；官方发行版签名只对维护者密钥签署的发行版有效（见 docs/release-signing.md）。
- 必须保留 AGPL-3.0 许可证与版权声明。

### 举报与联系

发现冒充或易混淆的名称，可向维护者举报（联系渠道由所有者在发布前补全）。
维护者可要求移除侵权的名称、域名与软件包。
